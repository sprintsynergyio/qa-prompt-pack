Story PAY-48: As an account owner, I want to be notified when a payment fails so I can update my card before my subscription stops.
Acceptance criteria:
AC1: When a payment fails, the user is notified immediately.
AC2: The notification shows an appropriate error message.
AC3: The account owner can update the card from the link in the notification; the page should load fast.
AC4: Failed-payment notifications are sent in a batch every 15 minutes.
AC5: After 3 failed payments the subscription is paused.
AC6: The system handles errors gracefully.
